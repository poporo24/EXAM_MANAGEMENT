Feature Spotlight: Cross-Institute Subject Aliasing

Solving the major vs. minor cross-college examination problem.

New Implementation

Institute 3: IEAS — Institute of Education, Arts and Sciences

Computing & Informatics

Code: ENG200

Minor Subject

How the System Handles This:

  • Single Unified Content Group: Grouped under content_group_id #1.
  • Slot Synchronization: The auto-scheduler treats all three exams as one unified group, locking them to the exact same timeslot.
  • Workload Constraint Preservation: For ICSLIS students it counts towards the Max 2 Majors/Day rule; for IBM/IEAS students it does not trigger the major limit.
  • Unlink Flexibility: Individual subjects can be unlinked at any time via the one-click Unlink button.
