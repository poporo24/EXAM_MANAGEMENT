Account Role Guidelines

Official responsibilities & permissions overview

Master User & Role Management

Exclusive authority to create, edit, activate, deactivate, and permanently delete accounts across all roles (Admin, Secretary, Proctor, Student), and manage Super Administrator privileges.

Dual-Authorization (Maker-Checker) Reset Authority

Direct executive power to approve or reject Schedule Reset Requests submitted by regular administrators, or execute direct master schedule wipes with automatic snapshot archiving.

Institutional Audit Trail & Security

Full oversight of institutional security audit event logs, IP monitoring, authentication tracking, and system-wide data integrity protections.

Full Operational & Scheduling Authority

Unrestricted access to all regular admin features, including automated timetable generation, curriculum catalogs, room allocations, faculty registries, and official reports.

Exam Scheduling Engine & Timetable Control

Configure examination dates and daily time windows. Run the automated scheduling engine with conflict-free placement across rooms, sections, and proctors.

Curriculum, Rooms & Subject Aliases

Maintain academic course catalogs, assign institute classifications, link cross-department common exam groups (aliases), and configure exam room capacities.

Faculty Workload & Proctor Availability

Manage teaching faculty registries, set proctor availability time slots, and monitor proctor duty hours to ensure equal workload distribution.

Dual-Authorization Reset Protocol

To protect published schedules from accidental erasure, resetting the master timetable requires submitting a formal request with justification for Super Admin authorization.

Exam Envelope Collection & Returns

Log and verify physical examination envelope collections by proctors before exam sessions, and record verified returns of completed exam papers afterwards.

Proctor Substitute & Reliever Dispatch

Immediately view reported proctor absences on exam day and assign qualified departmental standby substitutes to ensure no classroom remains unproctored.

Batch Processing & Envelope Tracking

Use Quick Single Collect or Batch Processing to distribute exams efficiently during peak morning and afternoon turnovers. Track reusable vs. non-reusable envelopes.

Departmental Rosters & Archive Access

Access official exam timetables, view proctor assignment rosters, generate printable collection reports, and look up past examination semester archives.

Duty Timetable & Room Assignments

View all your designated proctoring sessions, including building, room number, course subject, student section, and exact examination start and end times.

Assigned Proctor Availability

Review your institutional duty availability windows designated by college administration to confirm your eligible examination proctoring dates and time coverage.

Absence Reporting & Substitute Protocol

In case of unavoidable emergency or illness, file an official absence notice in advance through your dashboard so department secretaries can dispatch a relief substitute.

Official Proctor Assignment Docket

Download and print your official signed Proctor Assignment Slip to present at the College Secretary's office when claiming exam envelopes.

Personalized Examination Timetable

Access your complete, officially published examination schedule filtered specifically for your enrolled class section and course subjects, including dates, room allocations, and assigned proctors.

Student Workload & Rest Interval Protection

The CCA scheduling engine strictly caps daily section workloads to a maximum of 2 Major exams per day with mandatory 1-hour rest intervals between examinations to protect student wellbeing.

Building & Room Location Awareness

Check exact campus building names and room numbers in advance (Main Building, PAGCOR). Arrive at least 15 minutes before the exam period starts with your Examination Permit and valid student ID.

Live Reschedule Alerts & Permit Printing

Receive immediate system notifications if an exam slot, room, or date is adjusted by administrators, and instantly generate or print your official validated examination permit.
