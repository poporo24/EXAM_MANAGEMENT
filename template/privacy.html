Privacy Policy

CCA Exam Management System

Effective Date: October 2026

The City College of Angeles (CCA) is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for the CCA Exam Management System, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name (First, Middle, Last, Suffix)
  • CCA Email Address
  • Account Type (Student, Proctor, Admin)
  • Section and Institute Information

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Verifying your identity as a legitimate member of the CCA community.
  • Generating, managing, and distributing your personalized exam timetables.
  • Communicating critical updates regarding exam schedules and institutional announcements.
  • Securing your account through authentication (Firebase Auth / local database).

3. Data Protection and Sharing

We employ appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is strictly kept within the CCA network and third-party cloud authentication providers strictly for login security purposes.

4. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Be informed on how your personal information is being used.
  • Access your personal information stored in our system.
  • Object to the processing of your personal data.
  • Request suspension, withdrawal, or ordering the blocking, removal, or destruction of your personal data from our filing system.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact the CCA Data Protection Officer (DPO) or the system administrator.
