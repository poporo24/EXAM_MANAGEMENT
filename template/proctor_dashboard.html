Faculty Proctoring Guidelines

Official Invigilation Responsibilities • City College of Angeles

All faculty proctors are legally and academically designated invigilators. Strict compliance with examination protocols ensures institutional integrity and fair student evaluation.

1

Test Questionnaire & Material Claiming

Claim sealed examination envelopes, test questionnaires, and official attendance sheets from the offering Institute Dean's Office / Secretary at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time. Present your faculty ID or duty docket upon claim.

2

Student Admission & Permit Verification

Admit students strictly in designated uniform or approved attire. Inspect each student's official Examination Permit and valid CCA student ID. Unpermitted students must be directed to the College Registrar/Treasury for permit issuance.

3

Active Room Invigilation & Conduct

Ensure mobile phones and unauthorized electronic devices are silenced and deposited at the room front. Maintain continuous vigilant proctoring throughout the examination duration. Refrain from cell phone distractions or leaving the room unattended.

4

Turnover & Attendance Documentation

Collect all test papers, answer sheets, and booklets before any examinee leaves. Re-count all answered and unused test materials to match the student head count. Seal and return the envelope directly to the Dean's Office / Secretary immediately.
