Privacy Notice & Terms of Use

CCA Exam Management System

Please read and scroll through this notice before registering.

1. Data Collection

The CCA Exam Management System collects the following personal information:

  • Full name and account type
  • CCA Email Address
  • Section and Institute details

2. Purpose of Collection

Your information is collected for the following purposes:

  • To verify your identity within the CCA community
  • To manage and generate personalized exam timetables
  • To prevent scheduling conflicts and secure system access

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is securely stored using a cloud platform complying with international data security standards. Access is restricted to authorized CCA personnel. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

4. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account. Any fraudulent activity or attempt to alter exam schedules without authorization is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.
